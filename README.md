Hong Kong in Numbers — the official statistics of Hong Kong, plainly explained: population, economy, jobs, prices and housing. Every figure is traced to the Census and Statistics Department or the Rating and Valuation Department of the Hong Kong SAR, with its source and year shown on the page.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory app
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — no build step, no network requests. The site is static files under `app/` with hand-rolled SVG charts.
